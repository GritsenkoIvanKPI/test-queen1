## Concept
Turn queen-dress.com into one site for **QUEEN Fashion Group**: an international wholesale supplier that unites several wedding dress brands.
- Main goal: present the group, show all brands and collections, attract new wholesale partners (salons, boutiques, showrooms).
- Keep the domain, existing photos, collections and core content. QUEEN stays a full separate brand section.
- Feeling: elegant, premium, intriguing — "an atelier with a history". Modern, minimalist, close to the current site's style (light palette, serif + sans).
- References the client liked: Oliver Martino (collages, collection video, magazine feel), Yedyna (catalogue with filters).

## Audience and main actions
- Audience: wedding salons / boutiques (wholesale). Not brides.
- Main actions: leave a request; register in the partner area. Price request without registration (short form, 2–3 fields) + GDPR consent.
- Prices only on request.

## Catalogue and product
- Model naming: collection name + article (e.g. "Queen 2027 · 2747").
- Required filter: by collection. Client can also supply silhouette, neckline/back, sleeve, fabric, decor, train/length, colour, size range, made-to-order changes.
- "New" label for new models.
- Product page: front + back photos, video where available, article, short description (fabric, sleeves, train, colour).
- Size charts: Ukrainian and European.

## Wholesale terms
- Minimum order: 5 dresses from a new collection.
- Production: 6–8 weeks after prepayment; 8–12 weeks in season (1 May – 30 Sep).
- Payment: 30% deposit, 70% before shipping. Prices in EUR; payment also possible in PLN or USD.
- Delivery: DPD.
- Discounts/bonuses: none yet, open to developing them.
