About QUEEN Fashion Group

An atelier with a history

Handmade wedding dresses from the manufacturer, for bridal salons in 18 countries.

Seven bridal brands under one roof, made in our own production

QUEEN Fashion Group grew out of QUEEN — a wedding dress brand known for structured corset bodices, fine lace and long trains. Today the group brings together seven brands, from the signature QUEEN line to younger labels with their own handwriting.

We work with the owners of wedding salons, boutiques and showrooms. Partners order from our catalogues or ask for exclusive individual tailoring, and we take both large wholesale and small orders.

Our managers help each salon find its way through the assortment: which models and colours are in demand this season, and which payment and delivery method suits them best.
