About the group

An atelier with a history

QUEEN Fashion Group brings together seven wedding dress brands under one roof — from the signature QUEEN line to younger labels with their own distinct handwriting.

Every dress is made by hand in our own production, where each stage is controlled — from the choice of fabric to the final decoration. We supply wedding salons, boutiques and showrooms across Europe and beyond, and present our collections at Barcelona Bridal Fashion Week, Bridal Fashion Warsaw and Fiera Milano.
