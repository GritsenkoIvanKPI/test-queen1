Questions from salons

What is the minimum order?

Five dresses from a new collection. You can combine models and brands in one order.

How long does production take?

6–8 weeks after prepayment. In the high season, from 1 May to 30 September, it takes 8–12 weeks.

How does payment work?

A 30% deposit when you place the order and the remaining 70% before shipping. Prices are in EUR; you can also pay in PLN or USD.

How are dresses delivered?

We ship with DPD, door-to-door to your salon.

Can a model be changed for my client?

Yes. Sleeves, length, train and colour can be adjusted, and our designers can create a dress to an individual request.

Which size charts do you use?

We work with European and Ukrainian size charts.

How do I see prices?

Prices are shared on request. Send the form and a manager will reply with the price list within one business day.
