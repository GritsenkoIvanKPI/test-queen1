Modern style with delicate details

Elle combines modern style with delicate details. The line moves between minimal satin silhouettes and sheer corset bodices covered in soft lace.

Many models come with detachable sleeves or overskirts, so one dress can be styled in more than one way — a strong point for salons with a varied clientele.
